Copilot Field Guide

Microsoft 365 Copilot and Microsoft 365 Premium, explained for someone who already runs Claude Code every day and has never opened Copilot on purpose.

Ten short chapters, each with a self-marking check. Everything here was read from Microsoft's own pages between 8 and 13 October 2026, and dated where it matters. Mac marks the personal Premium plan you practise on at home. Work marks the Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on at your job.

Chapter 1Two products, one name

The single most useful fact: the Copilot on your Mac and the Copilot at work are different products that share a brand. Neither can see the other's data, and each has features the other lacks.

Mac Microsoft 365 PremiumWork Microsoft 365 Copilot
Sign in withPersonal Microsoft accountWork account (Entra)
What it isA home subscription for 1 to 6 people. Copilot in the Office apps, the Copilot app, Researcher, the highest home usage limits. AI features are for the account owner only.An add-on licence on top of a business Microsoft 365 plan. Three levels, below.
Grounded inThe web, plus files you upload or have openAt the top level: web plus your email, files, meetings, calendar and Teams, automatically
Price19.99 USD a month in the US at launchSet by your employer

Sources: Introducing Microsoft 365 Premium, Meet Microsoft 365 Premium.

The three work licence levels

At work, the label under your name at copilot.cloud.microsoft tells you which one you have.

LabelSeesCopilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote
Copilot Chat (Basic)Web data. Work content only if you paste, upload, or have it open in Outlook or Teams.No
M365 Copilot (Basic)Same as aboveYes, standard priority (slower at busy times)

Microsoft Graph is Microsoft's map of everything at work: mail, files, meetings, who reports to whom. Work IQ is the layer that lets Copilot and agents reason over that map, and it can be switched on or off. Semantic indexing sits underneath and makes search understand meaning rather than exact words.

Source: What is Microsoft Copilot? (Microsoft Learn)

You already know this as: a chat model with no connectors (Basic) versus a model with an MCP connector to your whole workspace (Premium).

Check 1. Which licence reads your work email and files without you uploading them?

Only the work add-on's top level grounds in Graph and Work IQ automatically. The home plan shares the word Premium but never sees work data.

Chapter 2The four doors

Every feature in this guide opens through one of four doors. Learn the doors and the rest is navigation.

Door 1 · both

Chat

Copilot Chat with a model picker (Auto, Quick response, Think deeper) and an Agents rail where Researcher and Analyst live. Opens at copilot.cloud.microsoft, in the Copilot app on Mac, web and phone, in Edge, and inside Outlook and Teams.

Door 2 · both

The app you are in

Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote and Teams. Draft, rewrite, summarize, analyze, recap. The Copilot button sits on the Home ribbon.

Door 3 · both

Cowork

The agent that does things: sends email, schedules meetings, builds files, posts in Teams, runs tasks on a schedule. It pauses for your approval before sensitive steps.

Door 4 · work

The builders

Agent builder (no code, inside Chat) and Copilot Studio (low code, at copilotstudio.microsoft.com). Where your own agents get made.

Two more places you will meet: Pages, an editable shared canvas that chat output can be sent to, and Notebooks, a space that holds a set of sources and lets you chat over them, with auto notes and mind maps. Notebooks has a light version in the Copilot app and a full one in OneNote.

Sources: What is Microsoft Copilot?, Cowork overview, What's New in Microsoft Copilot, September 2026.

You already know this as: Pages is an artifact. Notebooks is NotebookLM, which is where the podcast this page accompanies was made.

Check 2. Which door is the one that acts rather than answers?

Chat and in-app Copilot answer and draft. Copilot Studio makes agents. Cowork is the one that sends, schedules and posts on your behalf.

Early-access extras on the home plan Mac

  • Office Agent: one prompt produces a document, a spreadsheet or a presentation.
  • Agent Mode in Excel and Word: expert-level output from a single prompt, iterating on the file itself.

Prompt craft, in one line. Microsoft's own tip for Researcher applies everywhere: say what the output is for. Not "write a report for my review" but "write a report on my impact on the Contoso project for my performance review." Specific scope, specific audience, specific format.

Check 3. Copilot in Excel on the home plan needs the file to be where?

Microsoft's Premium page states Copilot in Excel requires AutoSave and a file saved to OneDrive. A local file shows no Copilot.

Chapter 4Researcher and Analyst

Microsoft ships two reasoning agents inside Chat. Both live under Agents.

Researcher both

A multistep research agent. You type a topic or question. It may ask clarifying questions; answer them, or just say "Go ahead". It then returns a structured report with key insights, cited sources and next steps, sometimes with charts. At work it can draw on your files, emails, meetings and chats, under the same permissions you already have, and you can scope it to work sources, the web, or both.

  • Model choice: pick GPT or Claude from a dropdown. Claude needs your admin to allow Anthropic models first.
  • Model Council runs the same question through several reasoning agents at once. Computer Use lets Researcher browse sites and handle sign-ins. Both are Frontier, Microsoft's early-access program for work tenants.
  • Researcher also runs inside a Notebook.

Sources: Get started with Researcher, What is Researcher Agent.

Analyst

The data-analysis agent. It reasons over spreadsheets and data and turns raw numbers into findings. Microsoft listed it as "coming soon" for the home plan at the October 2025 launch, so check the Agents rail on the Mac before planning around it. At work it depends on your admin having enabled it.

Source: Meet Microsoft 365 Premium.

You already know this as: Researcher is a deep-research mode. Analyst is a code-interpreter tool.

Check 4. What does Researcher do when your question is vague?

It asks follow-up questions to narrow the scope. You can answer them or say "Go ahead" to let it proceed.

Chapter 5Cowork, the one that acts

Cowork is the piece that maps most directly onto what you do with Claude Code all day. Microsoft's description: "Rather than describing what you could do, it does the work." You describe a task. It breaks it into steps, works through them visibly, and pauses for your approval before anything sensitive such as sending or posting. You can interrupt, steer, pause or cancel at any point.

What it can do

  • Communication: draft and send email, reply and forward, sort your inbox into folders, post in Teams channels and chats, send HTML newsletters.
  • Documents: create Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files from scratch, edit existing Office files in OneDrive and SharePoint without breaking sharing, create and reorganize folders.
  • Calendar: schedule meetings in natural language, move things, decline conflicts with a note to the organizer, daily briefing, meeting prep.
  • Research: search across the organization, deep research across sources into a report.
  • Automation: run a prompt on a schedule, or when something happens such as an email or Teams message arriving.

Skills and plugins

Cowork works by loading skills during a session. Built-in ones include Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, Email, Scheduling, Calendar Management, Meetings, Daily Briefing, Enterprise Search, Communications, Deep Research, Adaptive Cards, and App. You can write up to 50 custom skills of your own, which Cowork discovers at the start of every session. Plugins from the Microsoft 365 App Store add more skills and connectors, and an admin can deploy them for everyone.

The App skill builds a small interactive app from a description, no code, and lets you refine and share it. It is a Frontier preview.

Where and on which plan

Browser at copilot.cloud.microsoft, the Copilot desktop app for Mac and Windows, and the phone app. Generally available for work accounts (billed by usage). In preview for personal accounts, and personal Cowork needs Premium or Pro. Approvals carry a risk level for medium and high risk actions, and you can tell it to stop asking for a given kind of action.

Source: Copilot Cowork overview (Microsoft Learn), page dated 29 September 2026.

You already know this as: Claude Code, or Claude's own Cowork. Custom skills are SKILL.md files. Scheduled prompts are cron jobs. The approval step is the permission prompt.

Check 5. Before Cowork sends an email it...

Sensitive actions such as Send or Post wait for your go-ahead, with a risk indicator. You can skip future prompts for similar actions if you choose.

Chapter 6Building agents, no-code to pro-code

Stage 2 of your plan. An agent in Microsoft's vocabulary is a scoped version of Copilot with its own knowledge (instructions and data sources) and actions (things it can do), on top of an orchestrator and foundation models. The ladder from no code to full code:

  1. Agent builder inside Copilot Chat Work. Describe the agent, point it at knowledge sources, done. No code.
  2. Copilot Studio Work. A graphical low-code studio. You build three kinds of thing: agents (conversational, task-completing), workflows (drag-and-drop automations where each step can reason), and agent flows (the older Power Automate-style flow, attachable to an agent as a tool). It has analytics, evaluations with test sets, and an admin inventory. You can also describe a business goal in plain language and let it propose the agents and workflows.
  3. Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit in VS Code, plus Work IQ Dev Tools (a terminal tool, in preview) that scaffolds, validates, packages and publishes agents.

Copilot Studio's three harnesses

A harness is the engine an agent runs on, and it decides how it reasons and how it is billed. The GitHub Copilot harness for reasoning-heavy multi-step work. The standard harness for rule-based agents built from topics, which are scripted conversation branches. The Copilot chat harness for extending Copilot Chat with your company's knowledge.

Declarative versus custom engine

Declarative agentCustom engine agent
Model and orchestratorCopilot's ownYours (Azure, Semantic Kernel, LangChain, any model)
HostingNone needed, lives in Microsoft 365You host it, usually Azure, at extra cost
Proactive triggersNo, user-initiated onlyYes, can start work on its own
Group use in a Teams channelDesigned for individualsSupports groups, agent-to-agent handoff

Microsoft's rule of thumb: build declarative when the work stays inside Microsoft 365 apps and you want speed and inherited compliance. Build custom engine when you need your own models, complex orchestration, proactive messaging, or an existing assistant brought into Copilot.

Sources: Compare declarative and custom engine agents, Copilot Studio overview. Whether your admin has turned on Copilot Studio or Agent builder for you is not verified.

You already know this as: a declarative agent is a system prompt plus tools, hosted for you, like a Claude Project. A custom engine agent is your own Agent SDK app. The Agents Toolkit is the SDK plus scaffolding.

Check 6. A custom engine agent differs from a declarative one because you...

Custom engine means your model and your orchestrator, hosted outside Microsoft 365. That is what buys proactive triggers and group use, at the cost of owning compliance.

Chapter 7What already changed

Microsoft reshapes this surface monthly. Three changes matter for anything you read elsewhere:

  • 18 August 2026: Deep Research, Podcasts and Group Chat were retired from the home Copilot app. Researcher is now the research agent on both home and work. Group Chat threads and images were not carried forward.
  • Copilot Tasks moved into Cowork. Conversations migrated, Tasks memories did not, shared links stopped working, and scheduled tasks were switched off by default. Personal and Family subscribers lost Cowork; Premium or Pro is needed.
  • Names: "Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat" is now "Microsoft Copilot Chat", and the work address is copilot.cloud.microsoft. Older guides use the old names.

Keep the monthly "What's New in Microsoft 365 Copilot" post on the Tech Community blog as your drift check.

Sources: Copilot app updates for personal, work and school accounts, What is Microsoft Copilot?.

Check 7. Deep Research in the home Copilot app was...

Retired on 18 August 2026. If a tutorial says Deep Research, it predates that date.

Chapter 9The learning plan

Three stages, in order, agreed on 10 October 2026. Twenty to thirty minutes a day, one lesson per sitting, one real task at work the next day. Practise on the Mac, apply at work.

  1. Hours back each week. Email triage, meeting prep and recaps, first drafts of documents and decks move to Copilot and Cowork. Success: you can name which tasks moved and roughly how much time came back.
  2. Ship one agent. Built in Agent builder or Copilot Studio, published in the work tenant, used by at least one colleague for a recurring task. No-code first, then toward pro-code with the Agents Toolkit.
  3. Be the team's Copilot person. Demo it, answer questions, set up prompts, agents and workflows for others.

Official material to lean on

  • Copilot Academy Work: Microsoft's guided courses inside Viva Learning (Teams or web), with paths per app, per agent, and per role. Open Viva Learning, Academies tab, Microsoft Copilot Academy.
  • Copilot Prompt Gallery at copilot.cloud.microsoft/prompts: example work prompts to seed daily practice.
  • Microsoft Learn path "Transform ideas into action with Copilot": free course on prompt craft and getting started.
  • Copilot Success Kit and Trainer Kit on the adoption site: the material you will reuse in Stage 3.

Sources: Copilot Academy, Copilot skilling center, What is Microsoft Copilot? ("Train your users").

Work First task: find your label and prove the grounding

  1. Sign in at copilot.cloud.microsoft with your work account.
  2. Read the label under your name. Note the exact wording.
  3. Ask: "What did I commit to doing in emails and meetings this week? List each with who it was to."
  4. If it answers from your real mail and calendar, you have M365 Copilot (Premium). If it asks you to upload something, you have a lower level and the plan changes.

Mac Second task: one Researcher run

  1. Open the Copilot app on the Mac with your personal account.
  2. Chat, then Agents, then Researcher.
  3. Ask something you need for work, scoped and specific. Answer its clarifying questions or say "Go ahead".
  4. Note how long it takes and whether the citations are real.

Work Third task: one Cowork task with approval

  1. Open the Cowork tab at copilot.cloud.microsoft.
  2. Ask: "Draft a short recap of my last meeting with [name] and send it to them." Watch the steps appear.
  3. When it pauses at Send, read the draft. Approve or cancel.
  4. Then ask it to schedule that same recap to run every Friday. You have just made your first automation.
