The Copilots and where they live

One name, two products, three licence levels. By the end you can say which Copilot you are talking to, what it can see, and where to click to reach the agent you want.

Written 2026-10-10 from Microsoft's own pages. Microsoft changes this surface monthly; dates matter.

Why this comes first

You already know what an agent is, what grounding is, and what a tool call is. What you do not have is Microsoft's names and addresses for those things, and the one fact that trips most new users: the Copilot on your Mac and the Copilot at work are different products that cannot see each other's data. Every later lesson labels its task Mac or Work because of this.

Check yourself

Answer from memory before scrolling back. Getting one wrong and seeing why is worth more than getting it right by looking.

1. Which Copilot reads your work email and files without you uploading them?

Only the work add-on's top level grounds in Graph and Work IQ automatically. The home "Premium" plan shares the word but not the data.

2. Your home Premium plan's AI features belong to which sign-in?

Premium covers up to six people, but the AI features are for the subscription owner only and cannot be shared.

3. Which one sends the email itself, after you approve the step?

Cowork acts: it drafts and sends through Outlook, schedules, builds files, posts in Teams. Researcher only reports.

4. Where does Researcher live in the work chat?

Open Chat, then the Agents rail. Researcher can also be invoked inside a Notebook.

5. Deep Research in the home Copilot app was...

Retired 18 August 2026. Researcher is the research agent on both the home and work sides now.

Do it for real

Two short tasks, one per door. Each takes under ten minutes.

Work Task A: find your label and prove the grounding

  1. Sign in at copilot.cloud.microsoft with your work account.
  2. Read the label under your name. Note the exact wording.
  3. Ask: "What did I commit to doing in emails and meetings this week? List each with who it was to."
  4. If it answers from your real mail and calendar, you have M365 Copilot (Premium). If it asks you to upload something, you have a lower level and we re-plan.

Mac Task B: one Researcher run

  1. Open the Copilot app on the Mac, signed in with your personal account.
  2. Go to Chat, then Agents, then Researcher.
  3. Ask something you actually need for work, specific and scoped, for example: "Compare the three Microsoft 365 Copilot licence levels for what each can see, with a table, citing Microsoft pages only."
  4. When it asks clarifying questions, answer them or say "Go ahead". Note how long it takes and whether the citations are real.
